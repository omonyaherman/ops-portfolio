Bulk creator archive — 8,710 posts across 11 accounts

A pipeline that pulls every post from a creator account: video and photo carousels, watermark-free, each post filed with its caption, hashtags, an SEO title and per-slide hashes.

  • 2,631 posts in the largest single account; 11 accounts running on the same code with per-account config.

  • Daily schedule plus a watchdog that catches any run missed while the machine was offline.

Publishing lanes — Instagram + Pinterest through their own APIs

A content library scheduled and published on a fixed daily cadence through the official APIs, with a ledger that records what actually went out versus what was planned.

  • Per-platform limits respected by design, not discovered by getting blocked.

  • Metadata generated per post — title, description, hashtags — so nothing publishes bare.
